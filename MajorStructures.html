Major Structures

Execution of bridges, ROBs, VUPs, and reinforced concrete packages built for long-term durability and heavy operational loads.

Core Scope

  • Pier, abutment, and deck concrete systems.
  • Reinforcement, shuttering, and quality control procedures.
  • Seismic and load-path execution compliance.

Engineering Controls

  • Construction stage checks and pour sequence planning.
  • Survey and alignment verification for girders and bearings.
  • Concrete mix discipline and material traceability.
  • Integrated HSE and lifting plans.
