Who We Are

Over Four Decades of Engineering India's Toughest Infrastructure

Founded in 1985, Vraj Corporation is a fully integrated heavy civil infrastructure company operating across India's most complex geologies and terrains. We execute tunnel work, structures, highways, excavation, piling, and controlled blasting with complete ownership over project outcomes.

Zero-Harm Safety

HSE officers, toolbox talks, and site discipline drive safer operations.

Precision Engineering

Survey-grade control, geotech awareness, and method-based planning.

On-Time Delivery

Owned fleet and integrated teams reduce third-party dependencies.

Responsible Operations

Vibration and dust controls in sensitive and urban zones.
