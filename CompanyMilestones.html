Company Milestones

Four decades of growth in tunneling, controlled blasting, and integrated heavy civil execution across India.

1996-2008

Expansion into large road and bridge packages with growing mechanization capacity.

2009-2016

2017-2022

2023 Onward

Active participation in data center, energy, and transport mega-projects across India.
