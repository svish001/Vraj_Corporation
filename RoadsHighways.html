Roads and Highways

Mechanized civil execution for highways, expressways, ghat sections, and allied structures in challenging geographies.

Core Scope

  • Cut-and-fill balance planning.
  • Rock cutting and controlled blasting for formation.
  • Drainage, slope stabilization, and retaining systems.
  • Earthwork to specified compaction and grade tolerances.

Delivery Priorities

  • Corridor-level sequencing with milestone tracking.
  • Plant and fleet utilization optimization.
  • Traffic interface and safety controls in live corridors.
  • Coordination with designers and authority engineers.
